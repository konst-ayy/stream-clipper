Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Stream Clipper is a desktop tool that runs on the user's own Windows computer. This policy explains what data it handles when you connect it to TikTok.

What we receive from TikTok

When you sign in with TikTok and allow access, TikTok gives Stream Clipper an access token, a refresh token, your TikTok open ID (an app-specific account identifier) and the list of permissions you granted (video.upload).

How it is used

The tokens are used only to upload videos that you choose to your own TikTok inbox as drafts, and to check whether an upload was delivered or posted. Stream Clipper does not post publicly, and does not read your videos, profile, followers, comments or messages.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored only on your computer (a token file and a local database). Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's own API. There is no analytics, no advertising and no account with us.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone.

Removing access

You can remove Stream Clipper's access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) and delete the local token file tiktok_token.json from the Stream Clipper data folder.
