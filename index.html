🎬 Stream Clipper

A desktop tool for Windows that turns the best moments of live game streams into short vertical clips, and lets you send a finished clip to your own TikTok account as a draft.

What it does

  1. It watches a live stream on your computer and notices big chat reactions (laughs, "clip it", hype).
  2. It cuts that moment into a 9:16 clip with captions and a title, censoring curse words and nudity.
  3. You review the clips on a local page on your own computer.
  4. For a clip you choose, the Send to TikTok button uploads it to your TikTok inbox as a draft. Nothing is posted automatically: you open the TikTok app, tap the notification, add the caption and post it yourself.

How it uses TikTok

You sign in with TikTok (Login Kit) once and allow the video.upload permission. Stream Clipper then only uploads videos you pick to your own TikTok inbox as drafts. It does not post publicly, read your videos, followers or messages, and it does not share your data with anyone. Your login token is stored only on your computer.
